## What this proves, and what it does not

Proven behaviourally, not by string matching: the reported target is the holder's own and
survives a later environment change and a repeated `start`; an adopted/reused worker can be
verified at any time with `observe`; an unbound worker really does leave the Host unwoken;
and the documented recovery (keep the in-flight work, read it, exact stop/start at a safe
idle point, verify the fact) restores a working event loop, including `--resume`.

Not proven here: a pre-Issue #70 holder reporting `heartbeat_host_known: false` (covered by
the contract suite, since a live older holder cannot be conjured), multi-worker concurrency,
non-ZCode hosts, and PTY — all out of scope for this Issue.

Honesty note: every beat boundary except the last two was driven by an explicit prompt from
me as the outer Agent. What the Host chose for itself: verifying the adopted worker's binding
before being asked to, the recovery sequence it named, both acceptance verdicts with commands
it ran itself, `--resume` over a fresh thread, the exact worker stop, and the idempotent
handling of the terminated echo.
